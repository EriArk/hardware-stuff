# Bundled QR generator

`src/octofox_library/_vendor/qrcodegen.py` is the unmodified Python module from
Project Nayuki's **qrcodegen 1.8.0**, distributed under the MIT license. Its full
copyright and permission notice is retained at the beginning of that file and
included in the installed package.

- [Project and source](https://www.nayuki.io/page/qr-code-generator-library)
- [Release on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/qrcodegen/1.8.0/)
- Source artifact: `qrcodegen-1.8.0-py3-none-any.whl`
- SHA-256: `06bf87021bc351f1c6e722ac67db8a059c4fe27bbfbca39ed7a9a2364abdd7a6`

Companion creates QR images inside your installation. Connection links are not
sent to a third-party QR service.
